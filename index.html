The Scholars Got Something To Say

A South-Facing Dataset for Interdisciplinary Hip-Hop Research

5,826 works of hip-hop scholarship, with an emphasis on Trap and Southern hip-hop, gathered through 1,004 documented searches across five databases and journal hand-searches. Each work has its citation, a link where one was found, the searches that found it, and provisional tags for style, region, element of hip-hop and discipline.

Browse all worksSearch by keyword, filter, sort, and open each work's link. Browse by tagClick a style, region, element or discipline (trap, drill, Atlanta, MCing…). Browse by search topicSee what each line of searching found (Hauntology, Religion and theology…). Download and citeThe archived files on Zenodo: spreadsheet, CSV, database, README and codebook.

Before you browse: the browser tool takes about 20 seconds to open the first time. Citation details were checked by machine against Crossref, OpenAlex, DataCite and Open Library. The tags were assigned by AI, mostly from titles and search-result snippets, and have not yet been verified; treat them as a way in, not as findings. The README on Zenodo explains what has and has not been checked.
